Accessible application checklist

Use this prototype checklist before following a reviewed employer application link.

  1. Compare the required skills with the lesson outcomes and your own examples.
  2. Open the public certificate page and copy its verification link.
  3. Check the employer domain, closing date, salary details, and accommodation contact.
  4. Never pay an application fee or send identity documents before verifying the employer.
